## overview

This project was created as part of a course on cinematic direction in Unreal Engine. I was responsible for the entire cinematic production process, from finding and generating assets to scene assembly, material setup, lighting, camera placement, and final editing.

While preparing the environments, I searched for suitable models and modified them when necessary, including retexturing and material adjustments. I then assembled the scenes, worked on their composition, and set up the lighting and cameras.

Most of the camera staging and movement was created in Sequencer, where I arranged the shots and adjusted the timing of the scenes.

The last step was editing the footage into the final cinematic.
