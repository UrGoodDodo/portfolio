## overview

This project was created as part of a visual storytelling course and focused on developing the visual concept for a game.

The process began with defining the overall idea of the project. I described its character using three key adjectives, then expanded on them with several more specific statements that helped establish the direction of the concept.

The next step was defining the target audience. I identified its core group and selected two key archetypes to better understand who the project was intended for and what kind of visual and thematic choices would be most appropriate.

After that, I created a mind-map that broke the idea down into its main directions and highlighted the key visual manifestations of the concept. I then researched each of these areas in more depth, exploring relevant themes, imagery, and associations.

Based on this research, I assembled a moodboard that defined the overall visual mood of the project and helped bring the individual ideas together into a consistent direction.

The final stage was selecting more specific visual references for the main elements of the project, including the color palette, logo, typography, UI, characters, environments, and other parts of the visual language.

As a result, I developed a cohesive visual concept that evolved step by step from an abstract idea and key meanings into specific visual decisions.
